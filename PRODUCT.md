# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages from `github.com/Dechend-Z/KULABEEF` (branch `main`). Replaces the openlink.co/kulabeef link page.

## Users

Primary: export and B2B buyers (importers, distributors, restaurant and hotel buyers) evaluating KULA BEEF as a beef supplier, often outside Thailand, reading in English, Chinese or Arabic. Secondary: Thai customers who order packs over LINE or phone.

## Product Purpose

Give a buyer enough evidence to start a supply conversation: where the cattle come from, what is produced, how it is processed and certified, and who to contact. Success is a buyer contacting the Export Manager (LINE, phone).

## Positioning

Beef from the Thung Kula Rong Hai cattle network in Roi Et: a plain once known as dry and barren (2.6 million rai, ~980,000 rai in Roi Et) where farmers raise cattle alongside rice farming. Thai Wagyu cuts, processed by Nakhonphanom Beef (Thailand) Co., Ltd.

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive from a shared link (LINE, Facebook, Instagram, trade contacts), usually on a phone. Ordering and quotes happen off-site by LINE or phone; the site has no cart or checkout.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before adjectives: show certificates, the plant, and the cut.
2. Origin is the differentiator: Thung Kula, not generic "premium beef".
3. Every page ends in a direct human contact (Export Manager), not a cart.
4. Multilingual without degrading Thai.
