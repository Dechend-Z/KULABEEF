From dry land to premium beef

Thung Kula Rong Hai is a plain of 2.6 million rai across northeast Thailand, about 980,000 rai of it in Roi Et. It was once known as dry and hard to farm. Families here grow rice and raise cattle, and the cattle bring in much of the income.

The Thung Kula cattle network brings a new generation of farmers together to raise the value of that herd, so the families who raise it earn more from it.
